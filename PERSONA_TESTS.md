# Borrower Copilot — Persona Test Run-Throughs

This document records the three required persona run-throughs for the Borrower Copilot assignment.

The results below are based on actual application test runs. Values and wording are recorded from the generated assessment reports.

> **Important:** Borrower Copilot provides conservative decision-support estimates. It is not a lender approval or guarantee of eligibility, pricing, or sanction.

---

### Negotiation Card

| Negotiation Target | Value |
|---|---:|

> "I am comfortable considering around ₹8,00,000. Please quote an all-in APR around 10.25%–15% or better, with the EMI kept at or below ₹30,000 and all mandatory charges disclosed."

Priya has a relatively strong borrower profile:

- stable salaried income;
- established employment;
- strong credit score;
- existing EMI that is accounted for;
- requested amount below the conservative safe capacity.

The application therefore allows borrowing at baseline.

However, the stress test shows that the repayment becomes **tight** if household income falls by 20%, which prevents the assessment from presenting the loan as completely risk-free.

- reducing household expenses;

- no conservative capacity remaining for another EMI.

The application therefore prioritizes **avoiding additional debt** rather than calculating a maximum possible loan amount.

This is consistent with the borrower-safety objective of Borrower Copilot.

# What These Three Tests Demonstrate

The three personas exercise different parts of the Borrower Copilot decision model.

### Priya — Comfortable baseline borrowing

Demonstrates:

- salaried income;
- stable income;
- strong credit;
- existing EMI;
- requested amount below safe capacity;
- rate adjustment;
- stress testing.

Result:

**BORROW**

### Ravi — Borrowing should be reduced

Demonstrates:

- self-employed income;
- variable income;
- additional household income;
- unknown credit score;
- larger requested amount;
- conservative safe-capacity calculation;
- stress-test failure.

Result:

**BORROW LESS**

### Anita — Additional borrowing should stop

Demonstrates:

- informal income;
- existing multiple debts;
- high-cost debt;
- recent repayment issue;
- household financial pressure;
- zero remaining conservative EMI capacity.

Result:

**DON'T BORROW**

Together, the three run-throughs demonstrate that Borrower Copilot does not simply calculate the largest possible loan. It can produce three materially different decisions based on the borrower's financial situation.

# Observed Notes and Limitations

1. These are **actual application outputs** from the three test runs.
2. The reports provide final assessment outputs but do not expose every intermediate calculation.
3. The exact contribution of individual inputs such as Ravi's ITR income, household income, household expenses, and collateral cannot be established from the reports alone.
4. Unknown credit information is represented through a wider rate range rather than treating the missing score as zero.
5. The application distinguishes the requested amount from the conservative safe capacity.
6. APR is presented as an all-in comparison measure, but actual lender fees and charges must still be verified.
7. The assessment does not verify income, bureau data, lender policies, or collateral value.
8. Actual lender eligibility, pricing, and approval may differ from these estimates.
9. The rules used by Borrower Copilot are documented separately in `RULES.md`, including which values are application assumptions rather than universal lending requirements.
